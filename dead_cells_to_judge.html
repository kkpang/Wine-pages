How to read the panels column

Each year is one panel that flags the wine, coloured by the kind of flag. A wine flagged at every vintage of its region is the strongest removal candidate — there is no year at which anyone has seen it trade. A wine flagged at one or two vintages is far more likely to be genuinely dead at those years and alive at others, which is what the Côte de Nuits reopen found: 28 of 51 reopened cells priced.

What removal costs and saves

Removal is permanent and it is the only one of the two that needs your judgement. A removed row stays in the worklist file — check_alignment() compares the four worklists by position and observations store worklist_index, so deleting a row would silently re-key every cell below it. It is flagged, never deleted.

Availability is already your ruling

The 40 availability flags are not implied at all — they record a decision you already made, such as Mortet × Bonnes-Mares being a new wine from 2014. They are listed for completeness and need no action unless a ruling has changed.
