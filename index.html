Notes on this snapshot

  • Prices are ex-tax Hong Kong market offers (cheapest verified case, normalized per 750 ml, or loose bottle) pulled from Wine-Searcher on 4 September 2026 — a snapshot, not a live feed. Re-check before acting on it.
  • Quantity counts cases for case-held wines and bottles for loose singles; Bottles Left tracks what's actually still in the cellar as bottles get consumed.
  • For magnums and halves, "Per bottle" shows the real market price for that exact format when one exists; per-75 ml is the more reliable fallback when it doesn't (magnum/half listings are thin).
  • "Case" vs "Loose" reflects provenance, not current physical count — a single bottle pulled from a case and moved to another location stays "Case" (known storage history), separate from bottles bought loose from the start. Market Value is shown for reference only (this cellar isn't for resale); it's computed at the case-normalized rate for case-origin bottles, which in practice couldn't be realized by selling a partial case at that per-bottle price.
  • A few Terlano/Terlaner and Sauternes bottlings had no Hong Kong or UK listing; priced from the cheapest worldwide offer instead, shown tax-inclusive. The 1978 Armand Rousseau Clos Saint-Jacques has no market offers anywhere and is shown unpriced.
