Côte de Beaune red — calibration matrix

Every cell is market ÷ model, each vintage scored against its own model so the numbers mean the same thing in every view. 1.00 means the model is right. Warm = the market pays more than the letters predict; cool = less. The average view is the geometric mean of a cell's vintages — and it is the less noisy view, not a bigger sample: two vintages of one wine are two draws from one market. Disagreement between vintages is the more interesting signal. A corner wedge marks a cell carrying an exception — hover for which one.
