The two axes are not the same shape

Quality has nine rungs; prestige has nineteen. The prestige ladder carries a half-rung between every pair, written ^ — A^ sits between A and A+. Quality is judgement and moves only for a mistake; prestige is fitted by the calibration pass, which needs a finer step to converge. An earlier version of this page showed prestige at nine rungs and was quietly rounding the letters it documented.

The rungs are not evenly spaced

On the prestige ladder a half-rung is worth about 5.7% of price at A and 15% at the top. On the quality side one capability rung is worth roughly 100% and one cru rung 45%, so a single quality letter outweighs most of the prestige ladder — which is why quality is set by judgement and never by a price residual.

What is not on this page

Per-wine exceptions — a producer × cru pairing whose price the letters cannot reach — live in producer_cru_exceptions.md (quality) and producer_cru_prestige_exceptions.md (prestige) and are drawn on the calibration matrices, not here. Vintage is the third quality term and lives in vintages.md.
